INTERVIEW AUDIO CONDITIONS PACK

Purpose:
Synthetic English interview-audio stress pack for testing STT / intent / answer pipelines.

Format:
- WAV, mono, 16 kHz, PCM 16-bit
- 9 categories
- 8 clips per category
- Total: 72 clips

Categories:
01_far_voice            Simulated distant/room voice
02_slow_speech          Slow speech (~100 wpm)
03_fast_speech          Fast speech (~255 wpm)
04_interrupted_speech   Short audio dropouts / broken speech
05_very_long_sentences  Very long interview questions
06_long_sentences       Long interview questions
07_medium_sentences     Medium-length questions
08_short_sentences      Short technical questions
09_noise                Office-like noise/hum at varied SNR

Notes:
- Voices are synthetic TTS, not authentic human accents.
- manifest.csv and manifest.json contain the expected transcript for every clip.
- The acoustic categories use comparable AI interview questions to make A/B testing easier.
